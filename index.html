THE COLLECTION

I·MMXXVI

Barcaccia

After Bernini, 1627.

The iconic boat-shaped form of Bernini's Fontana della Barcaccia — reinterpreted through the ancient discipline of hand-laid mosaic. Every tessera applied by hand. Colours, patterns, and finishes composed entirely to a single brief.

MATERIALS

Artistic Mosaic · Solid Surface · CNC-Carved Marble

DISCOVER BARCACCIA

II · MMXXVI

Thermae

The Architecture of Wellbeing.

Born from the ancient Roman bathing ritual. A circular form — freestanding or built-in — designed to be the centre of the room. Surfaces in textured micro-resin or decorative cement, finished to endure.

MATERIALS

Micro-Resin · Decorative Cement · Custom Mosaic Inlay

DISCOVER THERMAE

III  ·  MMXXVI

Petra

The Sculpture and the Monolith.

Marble or travertine — chosen together with the client at the quarry, extracted, and carved from a single block. No joints. No seams. Machine-roughed, then hand-finished on every edge and surface.

MATERIALS

Italian Marble

DISCOVER PETRA
